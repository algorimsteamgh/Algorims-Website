The Problem

Your team keys data your AI could read.

Invoices, contracts, claims, and forms arrive as PDFs, scans, and photos — and someone still has to open each one and type the fields into a system of record. It's slow, it's expensive, and it doesn't scale with volume.

The Platform

Four commitments, in every deployment

Extract fast. Verify what matters. Deliver into the systems you already run. Keep control the whole way through.

⚡
01 · Extract

A tiered pipeline that only spends what accuracy requires

  • Textract-first, Bedrock-on-demand — foundation models are invoked only when Textract confidence falls short, keeping the cost curve flat as volume grows.
  • <45s p95 end-to-end for a 20-page document, non-HIL path.
  • Multi-format — PDFs, scans, photos, rotated and skewed originals, forms and tables alike.
🛡️
02 · Verify

AI extracts. Humans own the exceptions.

  • Hard human boundary — anything below the confidence threshold, even after escalation, always routes to a person. No silent auto-approval below the line.
  • Threshold governance — your named UAT business owner signs off the threshold before go-live; retuning ownership is written into the SOW.
  • Reviewer SLA agreed at onboarding so approvals never sit past a due date.
🔗
03 · Deliver

Straight into the systems you already run

  • HMAC-signed webhook delivery into your ERP or finance system — SAP, Oracle, NetSuite, or custom.
  • Full audit trail per document — extraction source, model, confidence score, reviewer, timestamp.
  • Searchable corpus — results return in under 2 seconds across a document archive of 1M+.
🔒
04 · Control

Predictable cost, in-region, with a way back out

  • Data residency — no document or extracted data ever leaves your AWS region. A headline commitment in every market, not a footnote.
  • Parallel-run + rollback on every production cutover, with a defined fallback if a document class underperforms the manual baseline.
  • Modeled AI spend + CloudWatch alarms baked into every deployment — cost predictability is part of the pitch, not an afterthought.
