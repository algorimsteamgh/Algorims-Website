The Problem

Generic chatbots answer confidently. That's the problem.

Your team fields the same questions over and over — RSVPs, order status, opening hours, fee reminders — on WhatsApp and email, one conversation at a time. The obvious fix is a chatbot. The trouble is most chatbots answer everything with the same confidence, whether they actually know or not.

That's fine for "what time do you open." It's not fine for "can I get a refund" or "waive my late fee." The moment a conversation touches money, a wrong automated reply isn't a minor bug — it's a trust event with a real member, customer, or resident.

  • ✓ Per-intent confidence thresholds — escalates to a human rather than guessing
  • ✓ Every money action is approval-gated, never silently auto-run
  • ✓ Works on the channels you already run — no app to install, no migration
Money Actions

We sell safe automation, not more automation

A wrong RSVP is annoying. A misdirected refund or an unauthorized "your fee is waived" reply is how a community org or SME loses a member's trust — sometimes real money. CXIQ answers the routine stuff instantly and stops cold the moment a conversation touches an account, a fee, or a payment.

The Platform

What CXIQ actually does for your team

Understand what's being asked. Approve what involves money. Connect to the channels you run. Audit everything, always.

💬
Understand

Intent extraction that knows when it doesn't know

  • Amazon Bedrock (Claude) extracts intent against a per-intent confidence-scored FAQ knowledge base — no ML training pipeline to maintain.
  • Config-not-code rules engine — your team edits what's automated without engineering tickets.
  • Repeated low-confidence extraction from the same person escalates to a human, rather than looping or guessing.
🔏
Approve

Money never moves without a human

  • Every money action is approval-gated until a rule is explicitly promoted to auto-run — no silent auto-payments, refunds, or invoicing above a client-set threshold.
  • Human-visible undo path on every automated action — RSVPs, reminders, and bookings are reversible by design.
  • Identity resolution is a defined flow, not an edge case — an unrecognized phone number never gets guessed at.
🔗
Connect

The channels you already run — no migration

  • WhatsApp and email from day one, with web chat and Amazon Connect voice escalation for the complex queries that outgrow a chat window in larger contact centers.
  • Language coverage scoped upfront — English plus Mandarin, Malay, Tamil, or Hindi as your audience requires.
  • Consent and opt-out are explicit and PDPA/DPDP-compliant by design, never implied by where we host.
🧾
Audit

Every action traceable, every escalation in context

  • Append-only audit trail for every automated reply and every human decision.
  • Escalations carry the full conversation summary — intents attempted, context, tool-call results — never a cold transfer.
  • Idempotency is an engineering non-negotiable — no duplicate RSVPs, no double-sent reminders.
Built On AWS

Two stacks, one product — sized to what you actually need

Most deployments only need the core build. A handful of enterprise contact centers need the heavier one. The trust rules don't change either way.

Enterprise — optional add-on

Contact centers with an existing CRM

Scoped as a dedicated engagement

  • Amazon Lex v2 for managed NLU on structured intents — order status, hours, account lookups
  • Amazon Bedrock Agents for multi-step tool use on open-ended queries — CRM lookup, product Q&A, in one conversation
  • Amazon Connect for voice — the escalation surface for complex queries that need a person, with full conversation context carried over, never a cold transfer
