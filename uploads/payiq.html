The Problem

AP toil is annoying. AP fraud is dangerous. You're carrying both.

Someone on your team is still opening every invoice, typing it into Xero or Zoho, and hoping they catch the one that's off. That's slow on a good week — and on a bad one, it's how a business email compromise scam gets a real payment out the door.

PayIQ takes the typing off your team's plate for the invoices it's sure about, and puts a hard stop in front of the one thing that actually causes AP fraud losses: a vendor's bank details changing without anyone noticing.

#1 AP Fraud Vector

Vendor bank-detail changes are how businesses lose real money

A convincing email, a "updated payment details" PDF, one distracted approval — and a payment goes to an account that isn't your vendor's. It's the single most common way accounts payable teams get defrauded, and it's rarely caught by looking harder at the invoice itself.

The Platform

What PayIQ actually does for your AP desk

Capture the paperwork. Guard the payment. Post where you already work. Stay in control the whole way through.

📥
Capture

Every invoice read, no matter how it arrives

  • Email, upload, or forwarded inbox — invoices are captured the moment they land, no portal habit to build.
  • Line items, taxes, PO references extracted and matched automatically.
  • New or unrecognized vendors route to an onboarding review — never a silent first post.
🛡️
Guard

The fraud stop that's never optional

  • Vendor bank-detail change = hard stop, always human-verified — the single most common AP fraud vector, closed by design.
  • Reversal workflow exists for anything wrongly auto-posted — a safety net, not just a gate.
  • Degraded-mode queuing — if the AI is briefly unavailable, invoices wait in line. They're never skipped or lost.
🔗
Post

Straight into the ledger you already run

  • Xero and Zoho Books, both live from day one — no waiting on a roadmap for your ERP.
  • Zoho markets get the deeper play: capture → post → bank reconciliation in one flow.
  • Reviewer SLA agreed at onboarding, so approvals never sit past an invoice due date.
⚙️
Control

Deployed into your own AWS environment

  • Runs inside your AWS account — your data stays in your environment, not a shared multi-tenant pool.
  • AI spend capped by design, enforced in the stack — predictable by default, not a bill that can run away.
  • Human boundary throughout — AI posts below the risk line, people approve above it, and that threshold is a decision your team makes at onboarding.
